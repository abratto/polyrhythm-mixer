Welcome to Alan's Polyrhythm Mixer
Layer two competing meters into a groove — tap in patterns, add voices, and shape the sound.
Pick a polyrhythm. Choose Meter A and Meter B (try 3 against 4), set a tempo, and press Enable Audio. A default pulse is pre-tapped so you hear the groove right away.
Tap steps and add voices. Click boxes in any lane to toggle hits. + Voice layers extra rhythms in the Master, Meter A Phrase, or Meter B Phrase lanes. Nudge shifts one voice; Nudge Group shifts the whole lane.
Extend with phrase lengths. Set A, B, or Master Phrase Length to 2–8 cycles for longer repeating patterns. When a lane has multiple cycles, use Cycle ◀ / ▶ to browse — the label shows AUTO (follows the playhead) or PIN (manual view).
Mix and share. In the Sound Mixer, swap instruments, solo, mute, and adjust volume per channel. Save stores rhythms locally; Share copies a link; Reset Mixer restores defaults.

Polyrhythm Visualization

Gears. The centre wheel has LCM(A, B) teeth and turns once per measure. Each side gear meshes with it and completes one full rotation every A (or B) teeth — spinning A× or B× faster than the master. Because each of those rotations spans one pulse, the A-side divides the measure into A equal beats and the B-side into B equal beats. Both share the same total duration but at different rates, and that simultaneous co-existence of two different equal divisions of the same span is the polyrhythm. Orange dots mark your tapped steps.

Master Cycle Timeline. One measure, left to right. Markers: orange (master), pink ◆ (Meter A wheel), cyan ◆ (Meter B wheel), red ▲ (Meter A phrase), cyan ▲ (Meter B phrase). White line = playhead.
